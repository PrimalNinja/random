# Cognitive Manipulation & the Firewall

*A consolidated model of persuasion levers, why they land on some minds and not others, and how to defend against them.*

**Working draft — model, not settled science.** Confidence flags are used throughout: **[SOLID]** replicated / well-evidenced, **[MODEL]** plausible synthesis but untested, **[WEAK]** overstated or empirically unsupported. See the note at the foot for corrections, removals, additions, and references.

**The frame of this document, stated literally.** This is an open-sourced, working document, intended to be contributed to — not a finished authority, and not a fixed position. It is versioned by its own changelog (see Corrections / Removals / Additions at the foot), it flags its own confidence and its own weak points, and it invites correction, extension, and counter-example. Reading it as settled doctrine would be a misreading the document itself argues against.

The frame is stated *literally* on purpose, because framing is one of the mechanisms the document analyses. Framing, subtext, and loaded words are the same family: each carries meaning that is not in the literal signal — a framing sets the interpretive container before the content arrives, subtext rides beneath the words, a loaded word pre-attaches a verdict (the substrate-vs-overlay rule of §3.1, and §10.1). A document about manipulation that let its own frame operate *unstated* would be running the very move it warns against. So the container is declared out loud: open, provisional, contributable. (An aside that is itself an instance: the word "framing" is not universally held vocabulary — where a person lacks the word for a move, the move is harder to question, which is the vocabulary-narrowing mechanism of §10.1. Naming the frame is part of the firewall.)

---

## 0. How to read this

This document treats offense and defense as the same content read from two sides. You cannot teach a firewall without naming the methods it defends against — forewarning only works when the specific trigger is named. So every lever below is documented completely: its mechanism, its tell, its counter, and who it does not work on. A half-documented lever (mechanism only) is a weapon; a fully-documented one (mechanism + tell + counter + susceptibility) is a firewall. The document is dangerous exactly where it is incomplete, which maps closely onto the sections flagged as unsupported.

## 1. Psychological Heuristics — social & ego levers  **[SOLID]**

These are the reliable core. They are standard Cialdini-plus-dual-process material: real, replicated, and — importantly — already public. Their novelty here is only in how they slot into the channel model below. §1 claims no originality.

### 1.1 Social Proof (tribal logic)
Truth judged by group acceptance rather than raw data. Simulated or implied consensus pressures the target to shift opinion to avoid isolation.
- **Tell:** "everyone knows / most people / experts agree" with no verifiable base rate behind it.
- **Counter:** demand the base rate. Specific, checkable data breaks the illusion of universal agreement.

### 1.2 Emotional Arousal (amygdala override)
High-intensity emotion — fear of loss especially — processes faster than analytical thought and narrows attention onto the heuristic. **Correction from the original notes:** this lever uses self-directed *fear*, not *sympathy*. Sympathy is other-directed and belongs to a different axis entirely (see §8 and §7).
- **Tell:** urgency injected to prevent you checking; a manufactured clock.
- **Counter:** forewarning. Naming the trigger out loud delays the impulse long enough for logic to re-engage. This is a replicated effect.

### 1.3 Consistency / Commitment (ego trap)
A strong drive to appear consistent to self and others. Tiny early commitments (foot-in-the-door) pressure larger later agreements to preserve self-image.
- **Tell:** a trivial "yes" solicited early, referenced later as leverage.
- **Counter:** if the initial commitment conflicts with a core value, reactance fires instead of compliance — the exit is to treat the small yes as non-binding.

### 1.4 Authority Deference (hierarchy conditioning)
Critical thinking offloaded to titles, uniforms, and confident delivery. Authority markers lower analytical defenses and input is accepted without checking what was omitted.
- **Tell:** authority that discourages scrutiny. Genuine authority invites it and volunteers its omissions; manipulators avoid transparency.
- **Counter:** ask what has been left out, and whether scrutiny is welcomed or punished.

### 1.5 Memetics (participation drive) — the appetitive twin of social proof  **[MODEL]**
The academic version treats the *idea* as the replicator and the human as a passive vector. The observed pattern is the reverse: the human is not infected, the human *wants in*. An idea spreads not because it is contagious but because participation is desirable — people reach for the thing that lets them be part of something. The stickiness is on the human side; the meme is just the current ticket, and its content barely matters, because swapping the meme lets the desire re-attach to the next one.

This is §1.1 turned inside out. Social proof is *avoidant* — match the group to avoid isolation. Memetics is *appetitive* — move toward the group to gain participation. Same force, opposite sign: fear of being outside versus desire to be inside. It depends on a **completable identity** — a self-model that is incomplete alone and completed by membership — and therefore does not land on a mind without one.

**Two populations, lopsided:**
- **Participation-driven (majority).** Content incidental; the meme is a membership ticket. Collapses the moment joining it buys nothing.
- **Content-driven (minority, rare).** The idea is genuinely intriguing and travels on its merits — the desire is to *spread the word*, not to belong. Survives even at social cost, even into an out-group.

- **Tell / test:** *would this still spread if joining it granted no membership?* The participation-meme dies; the content-meme lives. This separates signal from belonging without judging the content directly.
- **Counter:** ask what the participation is actually buying. The content of the meme is incidental to the membership it grants; strip the belonging and see what remains. (Note: what reaches a participation-immune observer is disproportionately the content-carrying minority — such a mind is a natural signal-detector, since the majority has no hook in it.)

## 2. Internal Cognitive Engines — active loops  **[MODEL, non-universal]**

The original notes assert every mind runs an auditory narrator and a visual engine, and that a lever must hit one or the other. **This universalism is false**, and the variance model in §7 is the correction. The engines below are real for the minds that have them — but their presence is not guaranteed, and a mind can log §3.1 structural data while generating neither inner words nor images. Treat these as channels that may or may not be installed, not as human constants.

**Framing: each inner channel is a spectrum from owned to disowned.** The two inner engines are not separate curiosities — each is the ordinary, *owned* end of a channel whose *disowned* end is a named clinical phenomenon. The axis between the ends is the one this whole document turns on: **source-attribution and control.** At the owned end, you know the content is yours and can start or stop it. At the disowned end, it feels external, fires without you, and cannot be stopped. Cyborg Unicorn names the owned ends as diagnoses in their own right — **subSchizo** (the self-owned inner voice) and **subPTSD** (self-driven inner imagery) — defined by the trait rather than by impairment: anyone who runs a self-attributed inner voice has subSchizo, anyone who runs voluntary inner imagery has subPTSD. A diagnosis is the identification of a condition from its defining trait; these categories do exactly that, drawing the line at the ordinary owned trait rather than only at the clinical extreme the established vocabulary reserves it for. The diagnosis follows wherever the trait is observed. (The only limit is evidential, not definitional: the framework can diagnose any mind whose trait is actually observed, but cannot assert an unobserved trait onto a person whose interior is not available — see the Commissioner note in Reference 2.)

**On the authority to diagnose.** Diagnostic authority is self-appointed in every case. The established clinical categories were not handed down from a ground truth — they are defined by committees of an appointed professional body, voted on, and revised edition to edition (categories appear, merge, and are removed over time); a government's legal categories are constituted the same way. In each case a body claimed the authority to name, defined the category, and the definition became load-bearing through adoption and use. Cyborg Unicorn's authority to define subSchizo and subPTSD is the same *kind* of act — asserted and defined by its author — differing only in scale of adoption, not in origin or legitimacy. What actually decides any diagnostic category, clinical or otherwise, is two things: whether it is **well-defined** (a clear trait and a clear boundary) and whether it **carves something real**. subSchizo and subPTSD meet both — the trait is directly observable and the boundary (owned vs. disowned, on the source-attribution axis) is precise — which places them on the same footing as the established categories, not below them.

*Note — the coinage is self-demonstrating.* These terms were coined in part to make, from the inside, the very point the document analyses under Authority Deference (§1.4): authority is **perceived**, conferred by assertion and adoption, never inherent in the title or the body that holds it. By minting two diagnoses and claiming diagnostic standing for them, the framework performs the exact mechanism it describes — and does so validly, because that mechanism is how *all* diagnostic authority is constituted. The categories are therefore both a real contribution and a live worked example: their legitimacy runs through perceived authority, which is precisely §1.4's claim that the authority was never inherent to begin with.

| Channel | Owned end (subSchizo / subPTSD) | Disowned end (established clinical term) |
|---|---|---|
| Inner audio / voice | **subSchizo** — inner monologue, self-attributed | Voice experienced as external (voice-hearing) |
| Inner visual / image | **subPTSD** — mental imagery, voluntary and driven | Intrusive, involuntary replay (flashback) |

The relevance to manipulation: a lever that pushes content into either channel is nudging it toward the *disowned* end — getting you to receive planted material as if it arrived on its own, or from outside. A mind at the far owned end (correct attribution, full control) resists this; a mind that already slips toward the disowned end receives planted content as more-than-its-own. A mind off the spectrum entirely — no inner voice, no inner imagery — has no owned end and therefore no disowned end to be pushed toward (see §7 and Reference 1).

### 2.1 Inner Monologue — subSchizo (self-owned voice)
Where present: an internal narrator and verbal logic processor. External triggers translate into self-narrative ("what does this mean for me?"), and targeted phrasing can steer what the voice repeats, which the mind then accepts as self-originated. This is the owned end of the voice channel — ordinary inner speech *is* self-owned voice-hearing; the same channel, with source-attribution broken, is what presents as external voices at the disowned end. Absent in anendophasia — nothing to dictate to, and no end of the spectrum present at all.

### 2.2 Inner Images — subPTSD (self-driven imagery)
Where present: mental snapshots and "movies." Loaded words generate conditioned images that reach the nervous system ahead of conscious appraisal. This is the owned end of the visual channel — ordinary voluntary visualization *is* self-driven flashback machinery; the same channel, firing involuntarily and locked to trauma, is what presents as intrusive flashback at the disowned end. Absent in aphantasia — no image forms, and neither end of the spectrum present.

### 2.3 The Cross-Trigger Loop
Where both engines are present: trigger → image forms → monologue reacts → emotional output, amplifying as the monologue panics over the image and generates worse ones. Broken by dissociation — watching the loop from outside rather than inside it. In a mind missing either engine, the loop cannot close in the first place.

### 2.4 Earworms (inner-audio infection loop)
A distinct inner-audio phenomenon: a tune lodges and replays involuntarily — "stuck in the head" — and some minds can replay it at some resolution of the original audio. This is a different source from the inner monologue (§2.1): the monologue is self-generated narration; the earworm is an infecting fragment that loops on the audio channel. The two connect where lyrics feed the monologue — a jingle's words or an advertising slogan can be carried in on the earworm's audio loop and then repeat in the verbal channel, which is why slogans are written to be sung. Absent where the inner-audio channel is not present: no audio loop to infect, and a jingle arrives as words on a page, not a tune that replays.

## 3. Implicit & Somatic Inputs — background engines

### 3.1 Passive Observance (environmental logging)  **[SOLID mechanism, MODEL as exploit]**
Preattentive scanning logs lighting, spacing, tone, and posture without generating inner words or images. Altered ambient conditions shift comfort, and the mind may misattribute that discomfort to whatever person or idea is present. Caveat carried from the notes and correct: habituation recalibrates in 3–5 minutes, so any exploit must violate an *expected pattern* (an anomaly), not drift slowly.

Further qualifier the notes lack: this exploit depends on ambient cues carrying a *conditioned affective load*. Colour (red = aggressive, cream = calm) and tone (warmth, menace) are not intrinsic to the signal — they are learned overlays on a wavelength, a surface property, a pitch. On a mind that reads the substrate rather than the overlay, there is no induced arousal to misattribute, so the mood-through-colour and mood-through-tone components are inert (the spatial-geometry component may still register as a physical fact). This is the same substrate-vs-overlay split that governs conditioned words (§10.1) and typography, and it extends into language itself: colour idioms ("red flag," "black and white," "green light") are the affective overlay *lexicalised* — frozen into the phrase so the valence ships pre-attached and reads as literal meaning. The firewall against all of these is one move — read the substrate, and require any valence claim to stand on the facts rather than be smuggled by the colour, word, or tone.

### 3.2 Somatic / Kinesthetic (body engine)  **[SOLID mechanism, MODEL as exploit]**
Physical feedback — tension, gut response, heart rate, breath. Many minds misread a physical sensation as a psychological truth (chest tightness = "danger"). **Stronger than the notes state it:** some minds route the other way entirely — low interoception or a propositional threat-response means the somatic signal is read as physical only, or does not present as a felt surge at all. For those minds this is not a lever.

### 3.3 Sub-Perceptual Priming (threshold engine)  **[WEAK]**
This is the weakest section and the notes half-admit it. The honest result: a stimulus loud enough to reliably drive behavior is no longer subliminal — it is an overt stimulus, which invites the very scrutiny the technique was trying to bypass; and a stimulus genuinely below threshold registers but does almost nothing. The window where an input is *both* undetectable *and* behaviorally potent is essentially empty. The broader social-priming literature largely failed to replicate through the 2010s. The "masking / refractory-window" patch in the original notes is a plausible-sounding fix for exactly this hole, but the reliable exploitable window it assumes is not established. Treat the whole engine as near-dead circuitry.

Note the definitional slide it rests on: "subliminal" is used to mean both *below sensory threshold* (the exciting version, which fails) and *below the threshold of noticing the influence* (the real, boring version — you are influenced by things you mis-attribute). The section borrows the credibility of the second for the claims of the first. Or, put plainly: subliminal until it is overstated, at which point it is noticed — and once noticed it is just an overt stimulus competing for attention, and losing.

## 5. Operational Layering — the 3-stage cascade  **[MODEL → WEAK]**

This cascade is the framework's original synthesis and is essentially untested. It reads as convincing because it chains real vocabulary, not because the chain has been measured. Each layer is flagged individually.

- **Stage 1 — Passive Restructure (environment) [MODEL].** Alter spatial geometry (e.g. seat the subject with their back to the entry). The passive processor logs the vulnerability and baseline cortisol rises unconsciously. Plausible; unquantified.
- **Stage 2 — Somatic Anchor (entrainment) [WEAK].** The claim that descending vocal fry slows the subject's breathing is not supported. Interpersonal physiological synchrony is real but weak, slow, and not steerable by a speaker lowering pitch on command.
- **Stage 3 — Compounding Linguistic Trigger [MODEL, unfalsifiable].** A vague phrase ("given the circumstances, we'll adjust") that the monologue grabs and fills with self-generated meaning. The mechanism ("dissonance forces the monologue to scramble") cannot be falsified as stated, and collapses entirely in a mind with no monologue to scramble.

Net: retain as an illustration of how levers *might* compound, not as a procedure. Its weakest link (Stage 2) is simply false; its framing link (Stage 3) assumes a channel that is not universal.

## 6. The Firewall — calibration as the core defense  **[SOLID]**

The strongest content in the document, and it does not depend on the attack model being true. The unifying insight: nearly every lever works by handing you a **wrong number** — an inflated probability or expected value — and hoping you do not compute your own. Social proof distorts P(the group is right); arousal inflates P(threat) and its salience; scarcity inflates P(loss); a false threat inflates P(harm) with no base rate; the priming claims distort your estimate of how much you can even be influenced. The defense is therefore one operation with many faces: **refuse the supplied estimate and compute your own.**

### 6.1 The deficit audit
- Name the environmental shift: "what changed in this room 90 seconds ago?" Externally naming it resets the passive processor and disables the misattribution loop.
- Separate body from argument: "do I feel this because of the chair, or because of the words?"
- Demand the base rate: "what is the actual probability this materialises, what is the evidence, what does each wrong guess cost?"

### 6.2 Why calibration is the general case
The audit in §6.1 is one instance of replacing an injected estimate with a calibrated one. A mind that natively runs its own numbers — the propositional route, trigger → judgment → action — cannot be handed a false one. This is also why the immune are immune (§7): calibrated probability applied to threat and persuasion is the propositional router doing its ordinary work. The metacognitive "observer position" — watching the loop rather than living inside it — is the trainable approximation for minds that do not do this natively.

### 6.3 Gullibility is a trainable dial, not a fixed trait
Gullibility is not a fixed property of a person — it is the *gap* between the estimate you are handed and the estimate you would compute yourself. You are gullible to the degree you accept the supplied number; you are less gullible as computing-your-own becomes the default. That gap is trainable, and the deficit audit (§6.1) is the training method, not only an in-the-moment defense. Each time you run it — name the shift, separate body from argument, demand the base rate — you are rehearsing the habit of not accepting a supplied estimate. Run it enough and it stops being a deliberate step and becomes the reflex; self-computed estimates become native, which is exactly what "less gullible" means mechanically. So the audit does double duty: it defends against the attack in front of you *and*, repeated, it lowers your baseline susceptibility. §6.1 is the reps; §6.2 is the trained endpoint (the mind that cannot be handed a false number); §7 is the same move named as training *toward* the immune architecture rather than only catching attacks mid-swing. The gullible reader's most useful takeaway is here: gullibility is a dial you can turn, not a trait you are stuck with.

## 7. Susceptibility & Variance — why a lever lands on some minds and not others  **[the actual contribution]**

The original notes catalogue levers but never explain variance: why the same trigger flattens one person and bounces off another. This is the part not written down in one place anywhere, and it is what the matrix in §4 actually acts upon. **Susceptibility is not a fixed human constant** — it is a function of which channels are present, how threat is routed, and how accurately states are attributed.

| Axis of variance | Why it decides susceptibility |
|---|---|
| **Channel presence** | A lever aimed at an absent channel lands on nothing. No inner voice to dictate to; no imagery to plant; no simulator to route sympathy through. This is *absence*, not mis-targeting. |
| **Routing: affect vs. proposition** | If threat runs trigger→feeling→action, the affective loop is exploitable. If it runs trigger→judgment→action, there is no felt intermediate to inflame. |
| **Attribution accuracy** | Most somatic/environmental levers work by mis-assigning the source of a state. A mind that natively traces a state to its cause never completes the misattribution. |
| **Anomaly sensitivity** | Passive-observance exploits need pattern violation, not slow drift. High anomaly-detectors notice the shift consciously, collapsing a covert cue into a scrutinised overt one. |
| **Reactance threshold** | Value-incongruent pressure produces compliance in low-reactance minds and pushback in high-reactance ones. Same lever, opposite output. |

The framework's three earlier gestures at variance each dodge this. The original §7 ("map to the target's primary system") treats variance as a *targeting* problem — find the right channel — rather than a *presence* problem, where the channel may not exist at all. The §3.2 alexithymia caveat notices one variance case and files it as an exception. And §2 assumes a universal inner voice and imagery that a real profile can lack entirely. The correct frame is the reverse: the ports vary, some are missing, and the wiring behind them differs. That is the whole of "some people are susceptible and some are not."

This also reframes the firewall. The strongest defense is not running the §6 audit under fire — it is architecturally lacking the channel, or natively routing through judgment and accurate attribution. You cannot fully install that in someone, but you can train *toward* it (propositional reframing, native source-attribution). A firewall built on *why the immune are immune* beats one built on catching the attack mid-swing.

**The source-attribution axis, in full.** The "attribution accuracy" row above is the pivot of the whole model, and it has three positions, not two — because each inner channel (§2) runs from an *owned* end to a *disowned* end. On the voice channel: content misattributed to an external agent (the disowned end — voice-hearing) · content correctly owned as self (the ordinary inner monologue, Cyborg Unicorn's *subSchizo*) · no verbal channel at all (anendophasia). The imagery channel runs the same way, from intrusive involuntary replay (the disowned end — flashback) through self-driven imagery (*subPTSD*) to no imagery at all (aphantasia). Susceptibility to the §2.1 lever — planted phrasing accepted as one's own thought — rises as a mind sits closer to the *disowned* end: a mind that already receives some inner content as external or uncontrolled will accept planted material as more-than-its-own, or as an outside voice. The far-owned end (correct attribution, full control) resists it, and the off-spectrum profile (no channel) has nothing to plant into at all. This places the three cases on one line: misattributes-to-external → correctly-attributes-to-self → no-channel, with exposure highest on the left and lowest on the right.

## 8. Fear — the underlying driver, and its decay  **[MODEL, self-consistent]**

More fundamental than §1.2, which only says "inject fear to override logic." This section says *what fear is made of* and — more usefully — *how it decays*, which gives a depletion order for the levers that run on it.

- **8.1 Fear of harm.** Directed at harm to oneself. Persists independently — it survives total loss, because one's own body is not a possession that can be lost. Resolves only when the *specific* harm is eliminated, not when other things are stripped away.
- **8.2 Fear of loss.** Directed at losing something of value. Depletes as the valued inventory empties: once nothing of value remains, this fear has nothing to act on and disappears. "Something of value" includes people — one's children are a high-value *entry*, not a separate category, and not sympathy.

**Decay order:** loss-fear is exhaustible (finite inventory), harm-fear is not (you always have a body). Personal physical harm is the last lever standing — the final nail once everything else is stripped. A framework built on fear should lead with this, because it tells you which pressure outlasts a target's resources and which runs out.

### 8.3 Two things fear is not
- **Not sympathy.** Both branches above are self-directed — about one's own exposure. Sympathy is other-directed (distress at another's loss) and does not follow the decay curve at all, because it tracks the other party's stakes, not one's own inventory. It was mis-folded into §1.2 in the original and is excluded here.
- **Not necessarily a feeling.** The valuation can run without the felt state: one can know a harm is against one's values and act to prevent it with no dread, no surge — a stored judgment ("this is mine, I disvalue its loss, I act"), reached by calculation rather than by feeling one's way into it. The car makes this cleanest: one does not want one's car damaged and will act to prevent it, while feeling nothing *for* the car. Same track for higher-value entries; the stakes scale, the mechanism does not. Where affect is present it terminates on the self, never simulated into the object. For such a mind, the "inject emotion to override logic" levers describe only *one* implementation, and the amygdala loop they assume is not there to hijack.

### 8.4 Overt pressure — coercion, blackmail, bribery
Three words people hear together and rarely separate. All three apply pressure to a *decision* in full view of the target — unlike the hidden levers elsewhere in the document, the target knows exactly what is happening and complies anyway. They are deployments of the fear machinery above (and, in one case, its inverse), so they belong here rather than in a family of their own. Because they are overt, the defense is **structural, not perceptual** — you cannot "recognise" your way out of a pressure you already see; you deplete it, externalise it, or remove its grip.

- **Coercion — comply or I inflict harm or loss.** §8 applied openly. Runs on either branch, and the decay logic governs it directly: loss-threat coercion is exhaustible (nothing left to lose → empty threat); harm-threat coercion is not (the body is always there → the last lever standing). **Directionally neutral — not inherently manipulation.** The same mechanism can serve the target's interest or the coercer's against it. Coercing a compulsive gambler to go and watch the swans instead of losing everything at the tables is coercion, and it is protective; pulling a child back from the road is coercion. The everyday case makes this undeniable: *"do your homework now and you can play with your friends later"* is coercion in full — conditional pressure attached to a decision — and it is good. Most people run this constantly and would never call it coercion, because the word carries a predatory connotation welded on (the §3.1 overlay: "coercion" ships a negative valence that is not in the mechanism). Strip the overlay and coercion is just *conditional pressure on a decision*, which parents, teachers, and anyone setting healthy incentives use all day. The predatory case (pressure that serves the coercer against the target) is the one people picture, but it is not the only one; the ethics are set by *whose interest the pressure serves*, not by the mechanism. Note too that this case sits on the coercion/bribery boundary: "homework → play" reads as offered reward (bribery's sign), "no homework → no play" reads as withheld reward, i.e. a loss (coercion's sign). Same arrangement from either end — which shows coercion and bribery are one conditional pressure viewed from the loss side or the gain side. Coercion is not generally illegal, precisely because it is directional: outlawing it wholesale would outlaw parenting, teaching, and every protective intervention.
- **Blackmail — comply or I release information that harms you.** A §8.2 loss-threat aimed specifically at *exposure* — the thing at risk is reputation, standing, or a relationship. Near-always predatory, because the leverage is a held secret used for the holder's gain (there is no benign "expose you for your own good" that isn't simply telling the truth openly). Its power is proportional to what stays hidden, so it depletes to zero the instant the information is out: the structural firewall is to have nothing exposable, or to release it yourself and empty the threat. This is §8.2's "once there's nothing left to lose" applied to a secret. Illegal in most jurisdictions — consistent with its being the reliably predatory case.
- **Bribery — comply and you gain.** The *inverse sign* of coercion: not "comply or lose" but "comply and gain" — pressure by offered reward rather than threatened cost. It is to fear what §1.5 memetics is to §1.1 social proof: the appetitive twin, same axis, opposite direction. The constant tell is that the reward is contingent on an act that serves the *offerer*, and is priced below what your compliance is actually worth to them. Structural firewall: price the compliance against its true cost to you, and note that the offer exists only because your compliance is worth more to them than the bribe. Illegal in most jurisdictions in its predatory forms — again tracking the ethics, while the benign "incentive" reading (the homework case) is not what the law targets.

## 9. Deployment Contexts — where the same levers show up

Propaganda, advertising, and threats are not separate mechanisms. They are deployment contexts for the levers already described, and they slot into the sections whose machinery they use. Probability is the exception — it is not a context but the firewall's underlying principle (§6).

- **Propaganda → §1.1 + §1.4 at population scale.** Manufactured consensus plus suppressed omissions. Firewall: demand the base rate behind "everyone / experts say."
- **Advertising / marketing → §1.3 + §8.2 + priming, legally constrained and repeated.** The one context where you *know* you are the target. Note the observer's floor: strip an advert to its information (this exists, it costs X, it does Y) and everything left over is spend aimed at channels — image, association, identity, felt appetite — that a mind lacking those channels registers as purposeless. That stripping *is* the calibration defense, run by default rather than as a technique. From outside the affective channels, most advertising spend is visibly aimed at nothing.
- **Real threat → §8 literally.** The harm is on the table. Assess: loss-fear (depletable — what do they actually control that you value) or harm-fear (persists until the specific harm is off the table). Response is assessment, not surge.
- **False threat → §8 + probability.** The harm is asserted, not backed. It works by making you act as if P(harm) is high when it is not. Firewall is not courage but calibration: the real probability, the evidence, the cost of being wrong each way.
- **Probability → §6, the meta-firewall.** Not a fifth topic. Every lever ultimately distorts a subjective probability or expected value; the general defense is to replace the injected estimate with a computed one. §6.1's audit is one special case of this.

### 9.1 Examples of Manipulation

Worked instances, each mapped to the mechanism it uses. (Organisations are left unnamed; the point is the mechanism, not the party.)

**Marketing — the grief-timed ad.** A funeral or funeral-insurance advert with sombre music, a soft voice, and a pretence of empathy or sympathy. The mechanism is §1.2 arousal aimed at a *temporary, predictable emotional state*: it preys on grief, a window in which the target's calibration is already degraded. The "empathy" is simulated — a projection into the viewer's loss to drive a purchase — which is the other-directed move §8.3 excludes from genuine self-directed valuation. Firewall: name the timing ("this is aimed at me *because* I am grieving") and defer any decision out of the window; nothing sound requires acting inside it.

**Marketing — the earworm brand ad.** A soft-drink advert: young, energetic footage, upbeat music, a hook engineered to lodge. Different target entirely. This is not aimed at an immediate purchase decision but at §2.4 — planting an earworm that carries the brand into the inner-audio loop, so the association re-fires later, unbidden, at the point of sale. It is brand awareness that *sticks* by infecting the audio channel. Firewall: recognise that a jingle's stickiness is not a signal about the product — the tune replaying is the mechanism working, not evidence of quality. (Lands on nothing where the inner-audio channel is absent: the jingle arrives as words, not a replaying tune.)

**TwinFaced — selective sympathy across equivalent cases.** An international organisation expresses sympathy or condemnation over an abuse by country A while turning a blind eye to the same abuse in country B — both members. The mechanism is manufactured moral consensus (§1.1) plus suppressed omission (§1.4): the selective attention manufactures a narrative that a *consistent* standard would not support. The tell is the equivalence — same act, opposite response, sorted by who committed it. Firewall: apply the case symmetrically. Would this framing survive if the parties were swapped? If not, the position is about the party, not the act.

**DoubleThink — omission and loaded words.** A claim built by leaving out the counter-facts and leaning on words already conditioned in the audience (§10.1, the substrate-vs-overlay rule of §3.1). The move presents a one-sided valuation as if it were settled fact, so the omission does the persuading while the loaded word supplies the verdict. Worked example (paraphrased, source unnamed): a public-health post asserts that *loneliness and isolation* raise the risk of stroke, heart disease, diabetes, cognitive decline, and premature death, and urges people to connect. The omissions and slides:
  - **One-sided valuation stated as fact.** "Lonely" and "isolated" are treated as intrinsically harmful. They are neither good nor bad as such — solitude can also raise focus, skill, peace. Presenting only the harmful reading is the omission; the words carry a pre-attached negative valence (the §3.1 overlay) that does the work a neutral definition would not.
  - **A universalised felt state.** The post assumes isolation produces loneliness, and loneliness is aversive, in everyone. Neither weld holds. "Isolated" is a fact about proximity; "lonely" is a felt lack; the post fuses them as if the first causes the second. There exist minds for whom isolation is a *preferred* state and loneliness simply never arrives — no felt lack in solitude, no participation-drive (§1.5) pulling toward company. Their existence is the proof that the negative valence is assumed, not intrinsic: the framing only works on minds where isolation produces a felt lack, and it treats that as universal. This is also why such a post's premise is invisible to those it does land on — they feel it is true because for them it is, so they never check it — and visible to those it does not, who read the claim literally and see an assumed universal dressed as a medical fact.
  - **Unfalsifiable counterfactual.** The mortality claim rests on a thought experiment — that a given person *would have* lived longer had they not been lonely — which cannot be observed, because the alternative life never existed. A correlation is dressed as a causal, individual-level certainty.
  - **Confounder conflated with cause.** If the outcome tracks *access to resources* (e.g. isolated people being far from hospitals), then the driver is availability, not the psychological state — building capacity where isolated people are would cut the risk without touching the "loneliness." The post conflates the two.
  Firewall: the general §6 move — separate the loaded word from its valence and demand the base rate and the mechanism. Ask what is omitted (the neutral or positive reading), whether the causal claim is observable or a swapped-in counterfactual, and whether the real driver is the named state or an unstated confounder.

## 10. Accountability Attacks — levers that target responsibility itself  **[MODEL]**

A distinct family the classical levers miss. §1 attacks through belief, §2–4 through channels, §8 through fear — none of them attack the *machinery of accountability*: how responsibility is assigned, deflected, or dissolved. All three below are redirections, but along different axes.

| Mechanism | What it attacks | The move |
|---|---|---|
| Accountability Terrorist | Whether accountability may be *sought* | Reframe the questioner as the aggressor |
| Blame | *Who* is accountable | Redirect the assignment to the wrong node |
| Checkboxes | *Whether accountability is real* | Dissolve substance into ritual that certifies without securing |

### 10.1 The "Accountability Terrorist" reframe
The move: recast the person *demanding* accountability as the aggressor — the questioner becomes a harasser, a zealot, a witch-hunter — so that seeking accountability is made too costly to attempt. It does not deflect a specific charge; it poisons the act of charging. Three layers:

1. **The mechanism** — reframe accountability-seeking as aggression.
2. **The delivery** — a conditioned trigger word ("terrorist") that fires a pre-installed image and felt verdict *before* appraisal. This is §2.1 + §2.2 firing on a single lexical trigger: the accuser need not argue you are violent, only say the word and let decades of nurture do the work. The connotation is not smuggled by the sentence; it is cashed in from prior conditioning. The attack is therefore pre-cognitive — it lands before any definition is consulted.
3. **The firewall** — interrupt the conditioned fire and go to the denotation. *Terror* is intense fear; a *terrorist* induces it. Violence is one **means** of inducing terror, never part of the definition — collapsing "terrorist" into "violent actor" is itself a factual non-existence (a not-defined property treated as the definition; cf. §3.3's definitional slide). Concede the literal charge and strip the smuggled connotation: yes, this induces terror; no, there are no bombs. That converts the accusation into a confession — the accurate, timestamped record induces fear *precisely because it is true*. If published fact terrorises the party being held to account, the problem is the fact, not the person reading it aloud.

- **Tell:** the response targets the legitimacy of the *questioner* instead of the substance of the question; it relies on the word's connotation, never its definition.
- **Counter / stance:** the label attached to the questioner does not touch the facts. *"I don't use bombs, I use timestamps."* Weapon: publicly provided information, cited accurately. The reclamation is available to anyone the trigger word was never conditioned into — for whom the literal meaning was always visible under the connotation that conditions everyone else past it.

### 10.2 Blame — redirection of the assignment
Distinct from 11.1: this accepts that someone is accountable and misdirects *to whom*. A targeting attack — the responsibility is real, the arrow is pointed at the wrong node. Scapegoating is the pure case.
- **Tell:** the assignment lands suspiciously cleanly on the most convenient or least-able-to-object target.
- **Counter:** trace the actual causal chain rather than accepting the offered endpoint — where was the decision actually made.

### 10.3 Checkboxes — dissolution into ritual
The subtlest: the checkbox *displaces* the thing it supposedly verifies. Performing the compliance ritual substitutes for the substance, and once the box is ticked, accountability is considered discharged whether or not anything real happened. Responsibility is not deflected to another person — it is absorbed into a procedure that certifies without securing.
- **Tell:** the artifact of compliance exists (form, audit, certification) but the property it attests is unverified or absent.
- **Counter:** ask what the box actually guarantees — does ticking it *produce* the property, or only the record of the property. This is the §6 principle made procedural: a ticked box is a supplied estimate of compliance, and the defense is the same — compute the real property, do not accept the certificate of it.

## 11. Meta-Observations

- These heuristics are *features* — evolutionary shortcuts enabling rapid social coordination — not bugs.
- Framing humans as passive "outputs" guarantees failure: subjects detect inauthenticity and develop reactance. An attack-first frame degrades the *defensive* reader for the same reason, which is why this draft leads with completeness and the firewall rather than with offense.
- The highest-leverage use is defensive and, in ordinary life, ethical persuasion — accurate expertise, accountability, community — not exploitation.
- **Removed as unsupported:** the VAK "representational systems" and submodalities material from the original §7 is NLP and has no empirical support. The claim that each person has a "primary system" a trigger must match, and lands on "dead circuitry" otherwise, was tested and failed decades ago. Aphantasia is a real perceptual variation; the NLP "primary representational system" is not the same thing and does not license the framework's inferences. The genuine phenomenon it gestures at — that channels vary and some are absent — is handled correctly in §7.

The subject who likes red is the clean proof the assumed mapping was never universal: "red flag = bad" is, for them, a non-sequitur overridden each time.

*General rule (one architectural fact, not a list):* across every channel that carries a socially-conditioned affective overlay — words, typography, colour, tone, facial expression, and language itself — the subject reads the **physical substrate**, not the installed connotation.
